MOMENTUM SNIPER V24 — KEEP CUT PRODUCTS FOR REVIEW

Every Product and Creator scan now writes:
1. PASSED products first, exactly as before.
2. A divider row labeled: THESE WERE CUT
3. Every rejected candidate after the divider.

New columns:
- scan_status: PASSED / SECTION / CUT
- cut_reason: exact reason(s) when available
- cut_tiktok_link: usable TikTok Shop link for rejected products

Examples of cut reasons:
- Avg price £7.75 (< £8)
- Commission £2.88/sale (< £3)
- Ads 6/10 (< 7/10)
- Brand/shop safety
- Commission/detail data unreadable

IMPORTANT:
Cut rows deliberately leave the normal tiktok_link column blank.
That means existing automatic Fashion Flow and Seedance handoffs continue to
send PASSED products only. If a cut product is close enough that you want to use
it manually, its link remains available in cut_tiktok_link.

Applies to:
- Product scans
- Creator saved-filter scans
- Specific Creator scans
- US market
- UK market
