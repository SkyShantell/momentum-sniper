MOMENTUM SNIPER V25 — COMMISSION-ONLY CUT REVIEW

Changes requested:
1. Removed the old restricted-product/name exclusion list across ALL Product
   and Creator scans.
2. "THESE WERE CUT" is now reserved ONLY for commission-dollar near misses.

A product appears under THESE WERE CUT only when:
- Avg price still meets the normal minimum: $8 / £8
- It was actually vetted
- Ads are readable and at least 7/10
- Brand/shop safety still passes
- Commission is readable
- Commission dollars per sale are under $3 / £3

Products cut for low ads, low average price, unreadable data, or brand/shop
safety do NOT appear in the review section.

Product-mode note:
The scanner now also runs commission-only near misses through the existing
AD/shop vet so it can verify 7/10+ ads. These extra checks do not promote the
product to PASSED. They are only used to decide whether it deserves review under
THESE WERE CUT.

Automation safeguard:
- PASSED rows keep the normal tiktok_link.
- Review rows leave tiktok_link blank and use cut_tiktok_link instead.
- Fashion Flow / Seedance automatic handoffs therefore continue to send PASSED
  products only.

Applies to:
- Product scans
- Creator saved-filter scans
- Specific Creator scans
- US
- UK
