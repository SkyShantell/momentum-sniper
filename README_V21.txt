MOMENTUM SNIPER V21 — CREATOR COUNT + DIRECT CREATOR SEARCH

Creator mode now has two source options:

1) SAVED CREATOR FILTER
   - Enter the exact saved Creator filter name.
   - Choose 1–10 top creators to check.
   - Creators are taken in Kalodata ranking order.
   - Each creator contributes up to the top 10 products on their Product page.
   - Every eligible product is then opened and strictly vetted.

2) SPECIFIC CREATOR
   - Enter a creator name or @handle, e.g.:
       @torijflow
       Tori Flowers
   - Sniper uses Kalodata's Creator search box.
   - It opens the best matching creator.
   - It scans that creator's top 10 Product rows.
   - It strictly vets every eligible product.

STRICT PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS ARE UNCHANGED:
   - Avg Unit Price >= $8
   - Commission earned per sale >= $3
   - At least 7/10 top product videos marked AD
   - Existing restricted-name / brand-safety checks

MULTIPLE CREATOR OVERLAP:
If two checked creators sell the same TikTok product, V21 outputs that product
once and records all matching creator handles in source_creators_all.

PRODUCT MODE:
The normal Product scan branch is unchanged.
